\begin{abstract}
We test an operational creativity triad---correctness, compression, and low
prior probability---in two finite spaces that permit exact enumeration.  All
256 three-input Boolean functions are synthesized in NAND, NOR, and
AND/OR/NOT formula languages.  Syntax-free semantic descriptors initially
predict minimum formula size with leave-one-function-out $R^2=0.831$--$0.920$.
The result survives stricter validation: after holding out all functions with
the same invariant descriptor vector, $R^2=0.613$--$0.656$, beyond 1,000 null
permutations matched on bias, algebraic degree, and symmetry ($p<0.001$ in all
languages).  Meaning therefore constrains synthesis difficulty.  It does not
determine a representation-free score: NAND--NOR rank correlations are 0.548
for minimum size, 0.376 for boundary surprisal, and 0.264 for canonical
compression gain.  Exhaustive enumeration of 221,832 correct four-input
sorting networks gives a complementary resolution test.  The 12 shortest
networks share one entropy curve but follow 12 distinct full output-distribution
trajectories.  At lengths six through eight, entropy distinguishes 27, 147,
and 497 trajectories, whereas the full representation distinguishes 756,
4,356, and 15,036.  Semantic structure is predictive, but apparent rigidity,
rarity, and compression depend on the language, prior, and observational
resolution.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Question and operational formalism.}
The motivating proposal assigns a candidate program $e$ a conjunctive score
\[
 \mathcal K(e)=
 \underbrace{\mathcal S(e)}_{\text{structure or function preserved}}
 \underbrace{\mathcal G(e)}_{\text{compression gain}}
 \underbrace{\mathcal R(e)}_{\text{low prior probability}}.
 \tag{1}
\]
The product notation is logical rather than psychometric: no factor is meant
to substitute for another.  The finite experiments make each dependency
explicit.  For a language $\mathcal L$, let $E_{\ell}^{\mathcal L}$ be the
ordered formulas or programs of exact length $\ell$, and let
$n_{\ell}^{\mathcal L}(f)$ count those computing semantic function $f$.  Define
\[
 L_{\mathcal L}^*(f)=\min\{\ell:n_{\ell}^{\mathcal L}(f)>0\},\qquad
 R_{\mathcal L}(f)=-\log_2
 \frac{n_{L_{\mathcal L}^*(f)}^{\mathcal L}(f)}
 {|E_{L_{\mathcal L}^*(f)}^{\mathcal L}|},
 \tag{2}
\]
and $G_{\mathcal L}(f)=L_{0,\mathcal L}(f)-L_{\mathcal L}^*(f)$ relative to a
frozen canonical compiler $L_0$.  Correctness is exhaustive over the complete
finite input domain.  Minimality and rarity are exact under the declared
grammar and the uniform prior conditional on length.

\paragraph{Boolean universe and semantic map.}
The Boolean universe contains every function
$f:\{0,1\}^3\rightarrow\{0,1\}$.  Semantic dynamic programming counts ordered
formulas by exact truth-table semantics through 14 gates; all 256 functions
are reached in each language.  Counts for single-binary-gate NAND and NOR are
checked against $\operatorname{Catalan}(\ell)3^{\ell+1}$, and the mixed grammar
is checked against its independent unary/binary recurrence.

The syntax-free descriptor $\psi(f)$ contains signed output bias, sorted
variable influences, Walsh--Fourier energy at degrees zero through three,
algebraic degree, and the size of the input-permutation stabilizer.  It is
invariant to renaming the three inputs.  There are 36 exact descriptor vectors;
their two-dimensional principal-component projection occupies 22 visible
positions.  Figure~\ref{fig:boolean-atlas} holds the coordinates fixed and
changes only the overlaid exact minimum size.

\paragraph{Representation stability.}
If complexity were intrinsic to the truth-table function, rankings would be
stable under reasonable complete bases.  Table~\ref{tab:stability} shows a
mixed result.  Minimum-size ranks are strongly, though imperfectly, aligned
between NAND or NOR and the mixed basis.  NAND and NOR agree much less when
the same function is held fixed.  Boundary surprisal is less stable, and
canonical compression gain is least stable.  The polarity of the primitive
operation and the compiler baseline materially change the numerical claim.

\paragraph{Stress test 1: group-held-out prediction.}
The optimistic leave-one-function-out estimate can leak semantic twins: an
input-permuted copy of the test function may remain in training.  We therefore
use seven-nearest-neighbor prediction under three increasingly strict
protocols.  The first removes only the target function; the second removes its
entire orbit under $S_3$ input permutations (80 orbits); the third removes its
entire exact $\psi$-descriptor class (36 classes).  Features are standardized
once without using synthesis targets.  Predictions are pooled over the 256
held-out functions.

\paragraph{Stress test 2: matched nulls.}
High prediction could merely restate elementary facts such as output balance,
algebraic degree, or symmetry.  Within each exact stratum of signed bias,
degree, and stabilizer size, we permute minimum formula sizes and rerun the
strict descriptor-class protocol 1,000 times.  The null mean and central 95\%
interval are $-0.064$ $[-0.271,0.118]$ for NAND, $-0.068$
$[-0.269,0.106]$ for NOR, and $0.014$ $[-0.149,0.155]$ for AND/OR/NOT.  No null
replicate reaches its observed $R^2$, giving the finite-simulation bound
$p=1/1001<0.001$ in each language.  Figure~\ref{fig:boolean-validation}
displays both the attenuation under stricter holdouts and the null separation.

\paragraph{Sorting universe.}
A four-input comparator network is a word over the six unordered wire pairs.
All 24 input permutations are evaluated.  No network of length at most four
sorts; exactly 12 of $6^5=7{,}776$ length-five words sort, giving 9.340 bits of
boundary surprisal.  The correct counts at lengths six, seven, and eight are
900, 16,800, and 204,120.  Correctness therefore becomes more common away from
the compression boundary.  Moreover, 8.0\%, 32.1\%, and 49.4\% of correct
networks at those lengths reach the sorted state early and append redundant
comparators.

For prefix $e_{1:t}$, let $Y_t$ be its output on a uniformly sampled input
permutation and let
\[
 p_t(y)=\Pr(Y_t=y),\qquad H_t=-\sum_y p_t(y)\log_2p_t(y).
 \tag{3}
\]
The first atlas represented a trajectory only by $(H_0,\ldots,H_L)$.  This
coarse statistic yielded one profile for all 12 optimal networks, suggesting a
rigid boundary.

\paragraph{Stress test 3: full permutation-state geometry.}
Entropy forgets which output permutations remain.  The refined representation
retains the complete 24-coordinate vector $p_t$ at every prefix.  A single
global wire permutation $\pi\in S_4$ acts on all $p_t$; a trajectory is
canonicalized by the lexicographically least member of this 24-element orbit:
\[
 [P(e)]=\min_{\pi\in S_4}
 \bigl(\pi p_0,\pi p_1,\ldots,\pi p_L\bigr).
 \tag{4}
\]
The quotient produces no additional mergers among correct paths because the
fixed ascending target anchors the wire order.  Table~\ref{tab:sorting} gives
the resulting resolution comparison.  The original rigidity claim does not
survive: the 12 optima have the same entropy curve but 12 distinct full paths.
Nevertheless the boundary remains small in absolute terms, and full-path
diversity grows rapidly with redundant length.

\paragraph{What the experiments establish.}
Three conclusions survive the stress tests.  First, semantic structure has
substantial predictive content for exact synthesis difficulty beyond trivial
symmetry classes.  Second, the values assigned to rarity and compression are
not properties of a function alone; they are conditional on a language,
compiler baseline, and prior.  Third, a compressed boundary can be both rare
and constrained while still containing distinctions hidden by a coarse
semantic statistic.  ``One trajectory'' was a statement about entropy, not
about computation in full permutation space.

These conclusions support a qualified version of (1).  The triad is useful
only with subscripts: $\mathcal G_{\mathcal L,L_0}$ and
$\mathcal R_{\mathcal L,P}$.  A credible semantic claim should additionally be
stable across a declared family of representations and observational scales.
The experiments do not measure human originality, discovery effort, aesthetic
value, or a universal Kolmogorov complexity.  They verify finite operational
proxies and show exactly where those proxies fail to be intrinsic.

\paragraph{Implication for formal proofs.}
For Lean, the result argues against interpreting isolation in one embedding as
mathematical profundity.  A stronger test would represent both theorem
statement and proof journey at several semantic resolutions, remove entire
equivalence neighborhoods rather than individual declarations, and require a
signal to survive vocabulary ablation, repository matching, and alternative
formal encodings.  The controlled universes demonstrate that such a semantic
signal can exist; they also demonstrate that its numerical strength will be
overstated unless representation dependence and resolution are tested
directly.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
All enumeration, invariants, group assignments, null draws (seed 0), tables,
and figures are generated by the checked-in scripts.  The Boolean synthesis
covers all 256 functions; sorting counts are independently checked against the
exact totals above.  The validation requires no cloud services or stochastic
model API.  The archived source essay and machine-readable CSV/JSON artifacts
are retained alongside the experiments.
